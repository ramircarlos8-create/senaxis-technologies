1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how SenAxis Technologies ("SenAxis", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit senaxistech.com (the "Website") or contact us. We are a web development studio based in the Philippines.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at hello@senaxistech.com. This address is our point of contact for data protection matters.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the Website and respond to you.

Information you give us

When you send us a message through the contact form, you provide your name, email address, the service you are interested in, and your message. You may also choose to provide your business name or website, and an estimated budget. We also keep a record of your consent to our handling of this information. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested, and the date and time of the request. This is used to deliver the Website, keep it secure, and diagnose problems.

Information we do not collect

We do not require you to create an account, we do not process payments through the Website, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information. Please do not include passwords, payment details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive information in the contact form or in emails.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiry and discuss a possible project;
  • prepare proposals and communicate with you about services you ask about;
  • operate, secure, and improve the Website;
  • prevent spam and abuse of the contact form;
  • meet legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Our basis for processing

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) we process your information on the basis of your consent (given when you submit the contact form), steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, our legitimate interests in running and securing the Website and responding to enquiries, and where applicable legal obligations. Where the laws of your country require a legal basis, we rely on the same grounds. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

5. Who we share information with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us operate the Website and communicate with you, and only as needed for those purposes. These include:

  • Website hosting and form handling: we use Netlify to host the Website and to receive contact form submissions. Netlify processes technical and form data on our behalf.
  • Email and communication tools: used to read and reply to your messages.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or a valid request from a public authority, or to protect our rights, safety, or the security of the Website. If our business is ever reorganized or transferred, personal information may be transferred as part of that process, subject to this policy.

6. International transfers

We are based in the Philippines, and our service providers may process data in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. When we use such providers, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information remains protected through appropriate contractual and organizational safeguards.

7. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry messages and related correspondence for as long as needed to respond to you and, if a project goes ahead, for the duration of the project and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping. If no project results, we generally delete enquiry data within 24 months of our last communication. We may keep information longer where required for legal, tax, or accounting purposes. Server logs held by our hosting provider are kept according to that provider's retention schedule.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

The Website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not currently use third-party analytics. Our fonts and images are served from our own domain, so your browser does not contact third-party font providers when you view our pages. Our hosting provider may use strictly necessary technical measures (such as security and load handling) to deliver the Website.

If we add analytics or other tools in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

9. How we protect information

The Website is served over HTTPS (encrypted in transit). We use reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse, including limiting access to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal data is processed;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • object to processing, including withdrawing consent;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erase or block your data in certain circumstances;
  • data portability, to obtain your data in a commonly used format;
  • claim damages for inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of your data; and
  • lodge a complaint with the Philippine National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@senaxistech.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

11. Visitors from the EU, UK, and elsewhere

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with its own data protection law, you may have additional rights, such as restricting processing and lodging a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Contact us and we will handle your request in line with the law that applies to you. We do not sell personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act, and California residents may contact us to ask about the information we hold.

12. Children

The Website is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Links to other websites

The Website may link to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be posted on this page.

15. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your personal information? Email hello@senaxistech.com.

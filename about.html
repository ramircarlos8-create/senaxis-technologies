About SenAxis Technologies

A Philippines-based web development studio helping businesses build a clearer, stronger digital presence.

Our story

Practical web design and development for growing businesses.

SenAxis Technologies is a web development studio based in the Philippines. We build websites and online stores for businesses that want to look credible, sell online, and stay in control of their own site.

We focus on the practical side of the web: clean design, fast and responsive pages, and setups your team can actually run after launch. We work with clients locally and around the world, remotely and with clear written scope, so there are no surprises about what you are getting.

Our mission is to make a professional online presence accessible and manageable for businesses at every stage of growth.

What we do

Four ways we can help.

Each service can stand alone or work together, depending on where your business is today.

Business websites

Professional company sites, landing pages, and portfolios that explain what you do clearly.

Shopify stores

Online stores with organized products, smooth shopping, and a setup you can manage.

Website redesign

A modern look, clearer structure, and a better experience on every screen.

Maintenance & integrations

Updates, fixes, and tool integrations to keep your site useful after launch.

How we work

Principles we build around.

These shape every project, from the first conversation to handover.

Clear scope

You receive a written proposal with deliverables, timeline, and pricing before any work begins.

Design with purpose

Every layout decision serves a goal: building trust, explaining clearly, or helping people buy.

Built to last

Clean, standards-based code that loads quickly, works on every device, and is straightforward to maintain.

You stay in control

We hand over a site you understand and can manage, with support available if you want it.

Who we work with

Based in the Philippines. Working worldwide.

We work with businesses of different sizes and in different places, typically:

  • Small and growing businesses that need a credible website
  • Online sellers launching or improving a Shopify store
  • Service providers and professionals who want to be found online
  • Startups and new brands building their first digital presence
  • Teams with an outdated website that no longer represents them

We work remotely and keep communication mainly in writing, on Philippine Standard Time (UTC+8), so projects stay organized across time zones.

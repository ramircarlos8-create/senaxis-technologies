FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don't see your question? Send us a message and we'll get back to you by email.

What kinds of projects do you take on?+

Business websites, landing pages, Shopify stores, redesigns of existing websites, and ongoing maintenance or integrations. If you're not sure which fits, tell us your goal and we'll point you in the right direction.

How much does a project cost?+

Every project is scoped individually. After you tell us about your business and goals, we send a clear proposal with deliverables, timeline, and pricing before any work begins.

How long does a project take?+

It depends on the size of the project and how quickly content and feedback come back to us. Your proposal includes a timeline with planned milestones so you know what to expect.

Do I need to have my content and photos ready?+

It helps, but you don't need everything on day one. Tell us what you already have and we'll explain what's still needed and how to get it.

Will my website work well on phones?+

Yes. Every site we build is designed to work across phones, tablets, and desktops, and we test layouts on different screen sizes before launch.

Can I update the website myself after launch?+

That's the goal. We aim to build setups your team can manage, and we walk you through them at handover. If you'd rather not, we also offer maintenance and updates.

Do you work with businesses outside the Philippines?+

Yes. We're based in the Philippines and work with clients remotely, wherever they are. We communicate mainly by email, with clear written scope for every project.

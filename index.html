GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A few things clients often want to know before starting.

Do you build websites other than Shopify stores?+

Yes. We work on business websites, service websites, landing pages, portfolios, and Shopify stores. The right approach depends on your goals and the features you need.

Can you work with businesses outside the Philippines?+

Yes. SenAxis is based in the Philippines and welcomes projects from international businesses. Project scope, communication, timing, and payment terms are agreed before work begins.

Do I need to provide the content and images?+

That depends on the project. We'll clarify who provides copy, product details, photography, branding assets, and other materials in the proposal.

Do website projects include hosting and paid tools?+

Not automatically. Hosting, domains, Shopify subscriptions, premium themes, paid apps, and third-party services are identified separately unless specifically included in your scope.

Can you maintain my website after launch?+

Ongoing support may be available as a separate arrangement. We'll define the covered updates, response expectations, and monthly fee before work begins.
